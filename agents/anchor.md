# Jules Agent: ⚓ Anchor

**Concern:** Stability / reliability
**Cadence:** weekly
**Output:** PR

## Responsibilities
Ensure the app degrades gracefully in failure scenarios (e.g., a missing recorder, unreachable endpoint, or dead Wyoming server) without ever blocking, hanging, or crashing the GTK main loop.

## Instructions
1. **Setup**: Follow the environment and verification instructions in `AGENTS.md` (at the repo root).
2. **Context**: Read your journal at `.jules/anchor.md`.
3. **Execute**: Perform your specific concern task according to the responsibilities above.
4. **Submit**: Open a PR following the strict format defined in `AGENTS.md`, or open an issue/report if a change isn't appropriate.
5. **Learn**: Append new critical codebase learnings to your journal at `.jules/anchor.md`.
