💡 What
Added strict buffer limits (64KB for JSON headers, 1MB for binary payloads) to the Wyoming protocol networking logic in `wakeword.py` and `wakeword_bench.py`.

🎯 Why
When parsing untrusted Wyoming server responses, unbounded buffer accumulation logic meant that a missing newline or a deliberately massive `payload_length`/`data_length` could cause out-of-memory (OOM) errors and loop forever, leading to a Denial of Service.

⚠️ Risk
Low. 1MB per chunk and 64KB per JSON header is extremely high relative to actual Wyoming protocol traffic (where chunks are usually less than a few kilobytes). Normal operation will be unaffected.

🔬 Verified
Ran `pytest tests` and successfully verified against the new `tests/test_security_bounds.py` regression tests checking for correct DoS drop-buffer behavior.

📎 Scope
`linux/blitztext/wakeword.py`, `linux/blitztext/wakeword_bench.py`, and `linux/tests/test_security_bounds.py`
