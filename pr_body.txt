## 💡 What
Enforces 64KB line length bounds and 1MB buffer/payload lengths on untrusted network streams parsing Wyoming protocols in `wakeword.py` and `wakeword_bench.py`.

## 🎯 Why
Prevents a Denial of Service (DoS) attack vector where memory exhaustion could be triggered by an attacker omitting newline delimiters or sending oversized `payload_length` values.

## ⚠️ Risk
Low. Legitimate Wyoming protocol wakeword events are tiny (kilobytes). Drops malformed frames cleanly.

## 🔬 Verified
Tested with `pytest tests` and manually verified bounded loop exit behavior.

## 📎 Scope
`wakeword.py`, `wakeword_bench.py`
